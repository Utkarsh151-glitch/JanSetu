How to apply for a certificate

Never share your OTP or password with anyone. Officials will not ask for it.

Read these instructions before you start. Applications are processed by the department in the order received, and you can check progress with your registration number.

  1. Keep a scanned copy of your proof of address ready before you begin the application.
  2. The fee is payable online at the final step; keep the receipt for your records.

The service is unavailable between 11 pm and 6 am for maintenance.
