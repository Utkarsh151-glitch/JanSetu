- Legacy AICTE evidence includes older guidelines linked from current NSP; academic-year applicability must be reconfirmed. Historical review dates are not source publication dates or live availability.

## Identity, ownership and duplicate limits

All service IDs, website IDs and exact operator relationships validate. All 25 old service IDs remain; 24 records are byte-equivalent JSON objects and PMEGP alone has a retained, digest-reviewed revision. Old publication decisions remain in the queue. The operator registry records exact existing website operators; responsible authority is separate department text, without invented department IDs or independent ownership certification.

Exact normalized title + department + level + state + district duplicates are rejected before publication. Shared login URLs are not duplicates. Explicit possibleDuplicateOf/duplicateOf ambiguity flags block publication. This detects strong matches, not all semantic aliases: similarly named certificates across states require human source review. No additional duplicate record was published in this batch.
